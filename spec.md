# Scuti AI Motion Graphics Video Production Brief

Reference inspiration:
https://x.com/everestchris6/status/2104231799090217078

## Objective
Create a real motion graphics video that introduces Scuti AI in a polished, modern, AI-brand style inspired by the motion design techniques shown in the reference. The video should feel premium, futuristic, and product-focused while remaining practical and visually believable.

## Core deliverable
Produce an actual rendered video asset for Scuti AI, not just a concept or mockup. The final output must be a video file exported locally in the workspace and should be at least 10 seconds long.

## Recommended creative direction
The video should communicate the following ideas clearly:
- Scuti AI is a modern AI-driven platform
- the brand feels innovative, intelligent, and scalable
- the product is built around automation, workflow optimization, and execution
- the visual tone is professional, high-end, and technology-led

## Visual style guidelines
Use a motion design system inspired by modern AI startup launch videos, with the following characteristics:
- dark cinematic background with neon blue / violet accents
- layered glass-like UI panels or HUD elements
- glowing central AI orb or data-core motif
- subtle geometric grid or futuristic technology background
- strong centered headline text for the brand name
- smooth motion, soft transitions, and premium pacing
- clean typography and minimal but impactful text blocks

## Required output requirements
- final file format: MP4
- resolution: 1920x1080 or equivalent widescreen HD
- minimum duration: 10 seconds
- final asset should be saved in the project workspace
- the video should be viewable without additional dependencies

## Production process requirements
Document the complete creation process in a clear written format. The documentation must include:
- tools used
- technical workflow
- rendering method
- actual prompt(s) or creative description used
- decision-making around pacing and visual composition
- final output link or file path

## Required documentation sections
The write-up should include:
1. Project objective and creative direction
2. Tools used for generation/rendering
3. Production pipeline
4. Prompt(s) used to guide the video concept
5. Final rendered output information
6. Personal opinion about the technique
7. Practical application to daily tasks, client proposals, or internal projects

## Technical implementation expectations
The video should be produced using a reproducible workflow such as:
- HTML/CSS motion composition rendered through a browser engine
- frame-by-frame capture and export to video
- code-driven animation rather than random manual editing
- a repeatable process that can be adjusted for future videos

This means the video should be rendered in a method that is clearly explainable and reproducible, not just a one-off visual effect.

## Acceptance criteria
The work is considered complete only if all of the following are true:
- a real motion graphics video is created and exported
- the video is at least 10 seconds long
- the final output is saved in the workspace
- the process is documented in a concise but detailed way
- the article clearly explains how the technique can be applied to real work scenarios
- the work reflects the reference style while still being original enough to fit Scuti AI branding

## Deliverables
Provide the following as final artifacts:
- final rendered video file
- a written project summary or article explaining the process
- a reference to the source files used to generate the video

## Final note
This is not a simple mockup request. The goal is to produce a real motion graphics promo-style video for Scuti AI, explain exactly how it was built, and show why the workflow is useful for practical business and creative work.
